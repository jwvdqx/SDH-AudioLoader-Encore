# SDH Audio Loader Encore 2.0.0

Spring cleaning: one look everywhere, fewer and clearer settings, and music that carries on.

### Added
- **Continuous Playback** (Music tab > Playback): the music carries on where it left off after a game,
  after the Deck sleeps, and after Steam restarts or the Deck is turned off and on (it remembers the music,
  the exact song of the pack and the second). While on, it goes before Time of Day, New Music Every Day and
  the shuffle modes when the music starts again; they still choose what comes next when a song ends.
  Disclaimer: it relies on how Steam pauses, sleeps and restarts; a SteamOS, Steam or Decky update may change
  that, and then the music simply starts from the beginning.
- **Find Music options**, compact dropdowns: **Per page** (5, 10, 20 or 50; up to 100 results), **Sort** (best
  match, shortest, longest, title, uploader, most viewed), **Length** (any, under 4 min, 4 to 10, over 10) and
  **Show** (everything, not added yet, can be added).
- **Listen before adding** (Find Music): plays the song from the site; the menu music pauses meanwhile and
  continues afterwards.
- **More sorting in My Packs**: name A-Z / Z-A, author, most / least played, recently added.

### Changed
- **One look everywhere.** The original Audio Loader screens were remade in Encore's style: the **Browse**
  page (pack cards with picture, badges for Installed / Update, stars; one row of search, sort, type and
  refresh; Load More) and the **pack details** page (picture, badges, stars and downloads, Install / Update,
  Star and Back buttons, description panel). Cards, badges and buttons of all Pack Manager pages now share one
  set of styles.
- **Fewer switches in the menu**, dropdowns instead:
  - Muffled Music: **Menus** (off, Steam menu, Quick Access, both), **Pop-ups and Notifications** (off, all,
    all but Now Playing) and **Volume and Microphone** (off, volume, microphone, both) replace six switches.
  - **Lower the Music For** (nothing, notifications, new audio device, both) replaces two switches.
  - **Battery Saver** (off, below 10/15/20/30/50%) replaces a switch and a slider.
  Your settings carry over.
- **My Packs filters** are one row of dropdowns (Type, Show, Sort) next to the search.
- **Smoother fades:** fades follow equal-power curves (the loudness stays even during a crossfade, and a
  fade-out no longer seems to drop off at the end), and they are timed by the clock, so slowed-down timers
  (a game starting) no longer stretch them.
- **README** rewritten: install, the menu, the Pack Manager, how options work together, and the disclaimers.

### Removed
- Leftovers of the original plugin that nothing used anymore: the old About page, the old button colouring,
  an unused style sheet, unused functions, and the editor setup for copying builds to a Deck by hand (builds
  come from GitHub Actions).

### Project
- New tests: Continuous Playback, the Find Music options and page sizes, previews, and the fade curves.
